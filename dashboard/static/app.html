📲 Install JARVIS App

Install JARVIS on your device for a full-screen standalone experience with direct mic access.

1
Tap the Share button at the bottom of Safari (the square with an arrow: ⎋).
2
Scroll down the options and tap "Add to Home Screen" (➕).
3
Tap "Add" in the top-right corner. JARVIS will appear as a standalone app on your home screen!
1
Tap the three vertical dots (⋮) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
2
Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
3
Confirm by tapping "Install". JARVIS will be added to your app drawer!
